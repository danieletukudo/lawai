## ARTICLE VI: EMANCIPATION DEFINITION

23. With respect to child support and expenses for the child, an emancipation event shall occur or be deemed to have occurred upon the earliest happening of any of the following and the parties’ obligation to contribute toward the child’s expenses shall cease upon:

(a) The child reaching the age of nineteen (19) years, or completion of four (4) continuous academic years of college or completion of vocational school, whichever last occurs, with the understanding that a parent’s child support obligation shall continue so long as the child diligently pursues a college or vocational schoo! education and is attending college or vocational school on a full time (defined as at least 12 credits per semester) except if there are health issues or exceptional circumstances.

(b) Marriage of the child, whether valid or voidable.

(c) Permanent residence away from residence of either parent. A residence at boarding school, camp, or college is not deemed to be a residence away from the residence of the Husband or Wife and such a residence at boarding school, camp or college is not an emancipation event.

(d) Death of the child.

(e) Entry into the armed forces of the United States, so long as it remains permanent.

(f) Engaging in full time employment or the ability to engage in full time employment upon and after the child attains nineteen (19) years of age except and provided that engaging by the child in full or part time employment during school or vacation and summer periods shall not be deemed an emancipation event.
