Client is Defendant. This is a working document, not a confirmed filed version.

I, Defendant, hereby certify as follows:

1\. I am resident of the State of New Jersey. I have resided at the marital home at \[Former Marital Residence\] in the Municipality A, \[County omitted\] since 2043.

2\. I make this Certification in support of this Motion for Pendente Lite support.

3\. Plaintiff and Defendant were married on February 7, 2033 in the Municipality C, \[County omitted\], State of New Jersey. A copy of that marriage certificate is attached as Exhibit A.

4\. During this marriage, the parties lived a joint lifestyle that included several vacations a year, fine dining, luxury shopping, expensive hobbies, jewelry, cars and club memberships.

5\. Parties regularly shopped at Retailers A through F, among other retail establishments.

6\. Plaintiff frequently bought and maintains clothing from these designers as part of his current wardrobe.

7\. Plaintiff maintains several expensive hobbies, including collecting recreational equipment and vehicles.

8\. Plaintiff maintains a membership at the Recreation Club A, a private racing club wherein the entry fee is \$200,000.00.

9\. Over the last twenty-one years, Plaintiff gifted the Defendant with over \$400,000.00 of fine jewelry; a coat; several luxury vehicles; international trips and vacations; a couture wardrobe, designer shoes and handbags, a custom-designed home, and a second weekend home in New Jersey, among other items.

10\. There were no children born of this marriage.

11\. To date, the parties have not divorced.

12\. On or about August 3, 2054, Plaintiff requested a divorce from Defendant. A copy of that email is attached as Exhibit B.

13\. On or about August 8, 2054, Plaintiff physically left the marital home at \[Former Marital Residence\], Municipality A, stating that “he would not return.”

14\. On or about September 9, 2054, Plaintiff engaged an attorney and informed Defendant that he would no longer be funding their joint bank account or their joint savings account. 15\. Plaintiff also demanded that Defendant reimburse him for purchases legally made by Defendant from their joint checking account.

16\. Plaintiff also demanded that Defendant sign and execute a draft contract that backdated his mandated spousal support to August 20, 2054. A copy of that document is attached as Exhibit D.

17\. My counsel attempted to remonstrate with Plaintiff and explained the theory of spousal support, indicating that such a move would not be appropriate. A copy of that letter is attached as Exhibit E.

18\. I accordance with Plaintiff’s payroll schedule, a deposit of at least \$16,302.46 from his corporate payroll was last deposited into the joint Joint Checking account on August 31, 2054. The next payment, falling on September 18, 2054, never occurred.

19\. Plaintiff filed for divorce on Friday, September 18, 2054.

20\. No further payroll deposits, or deposits of any sort, were made by Plaintiff since that date.

21\. Further withdrawals from the couples’ joint checking account were made by Plaintiff without Defendant’s knowledge as follows, leaving only \$33,885.62 in funds:

a\. \$4,415.46 transferred by Plaintiff to his private Bank C account on August 17, 2054;

b\. \$2,920.18 transferred by Plaintiff to his private Bank C account on September 16, 2054;

22\. The parties also maintain a joint Joint Savings account at Bank B. As of September 24, 2054, the balance was \$148,527.02.

24\. Should Plaintiff continue his dissolution of marital funds, Defendant has only temporary access to the declining \$108,650.64 that remain in both accounts.

25\. In 2053, Defendant earned roughly \$42,000.00 from her professional practice. These marital funds are necessary to maintain her lifestyle and support her during the pendency of this action.

I certify that the foregoing factual statements by me are true. I am aware that if any of the foregoing statements made by me are willfully false, I am subject to punishment.
