# Firm SOP summary

Team reference \| October 20, 2026

Use this summary to select the appropriate practice guide, prepare the
work, and check the completed submission. It covers discovery, motions,
contested custody, trials, and attorney advertising. The full guides
supply the governing qualifications, exceptions, and supporting
authorities.

## B. Controls used throughout the guides

1\. Identify the docket, procedural posture, disputed issues, existing
orders, and specific relief before choosing a form or procedure.

2\. Calendar the applicable rule deadlines and case-specific dates.
Confirm any extension, adjournment, or changed schedule with the court.

3\. Connect each requested result to the required legal showing,
competent evidence, and a workable proposed order.

4\. Review confidentiality, privilege, redactions, signatures, service,
and filing requirements before release. Preserve the final submission
and proof of service.

5\. After a ruling, obtain the signed order and reasons, explain the
obligations to the client, and calendar implementation and review
deadlines.

## C. How to use this summary

The guides distinguish court requirements from recommended management
practices. Check the controlling rule, specialized proceeding, and
assigned judge's directions before acting. This summary is a navigation
and completion aid; detailed deadlines and exceptions remain in the full
guides.

## Discovery Form Selection

### A. Select the correct direction and scope

Use Plaintiff to Defendant when we represent Plaintiff and Defendant to
Plaintiff when we represent Defendant. Select demands by the issues
requiring proof. Remove irrelevant questions, tailor date ranges, and
avoid duplicating requests already answered.

### B. Select the notice to produce

General Matrimonial: routine prejudgment financial records, including
basic business ownership and separate-property support. Business and
Professional Practice: add for valuation, detailed income review,
business-paid personal expenses, or related-party transactions.
Premarital and Other Claimed Separate Assets: add for historical tracing
of premarital property, gifts, inheritances, appreciation, or claimed
credits.

### C. Before service and after response

Complete the caption, counsel details, service and response dates,
production method, and controlling order. Confirm authority to obtain
discovery; review scope, numbering, and certifications. Track an answer
to each item, specific objections, document references, missing records,
and supplementation. The responding person supplies the required
completeness certification.

The production forms are not dedicated postjudgment, custody,
disability, or Tevis production sets. Use the full guides and an
appropriate tailored form for those matters.

## Discovery Management

### A. Plan and preserve

1\. Identify the fact to prove, why it matters, the best source, the
authorized discovery device, and the completion deadline. Summary and
postjudgment matters require a separate authority and threshold
analysis.

2\. Identify relevant devices, accounts, messages, cloud records, and
business systems. Give tailored preservation instructions and address
automatic deletion. Collect only through lawful access.

3\. Work backward from discovery completion to allow responses,
deficiency review, expert work, and a timely court application. A demand
served on the last day does not complete discovery.

### B. Collect and review

1\. Tailor categories and periods. Distinguish a party document demand
from nonparty subpoena process; observe notice, objection, and release
protections.

2\. Check every interrogatory answer for responsiveness, verification,
relevant periods, attachments, and precise Case Information Statement
(CIS) references. A stack of records does not replace answers.

3\. Check document responses against each request and the required
completeness certification. Maintain a stable production index and the
exact set produced; track supplements.

4\. Reconcile income, transfers, accounts, expenses, and claimed
credits. Avoid counting transfers as new income or counting the same
asset twice.

5\. Identify privilege and confidentiality issues separately. Use
focused protocols for sensitive records or forensic access. Preserve
usable native files, message context, and necessary metadata.

### C. Resolve deficiencies and close discovery

Maintain a deficiency tracker identifying the request, due date,
response, missing material, resolution efforts, and requested remedy.
Complete the required consultation or warning procedure before moving.
Seek relief while a timely return date remains available.

Distinguish a motion for more specific answers or production from staged
dismissal or suppression procedures. Follow each procedure's notice,
cure, restoration, and appearance protections; request a remedy
proportionate to the actual violation.

Before discovery closes, reconcile answers, production, the CIS, expert
reports, outstanding subpoenas, and promised supplements. Obtain an
actual revised order if more time is needed. For trial, identify the
authenticating witness, hearsay basis, privilege ruling, and other
foundation for each exhibit. Production alone does not establish
admissibility.

## Motion Preparation and Follow Through

### A. Select the application and calendar it

Distinguish enforcement, modification, reconsideration, relief from
judgment, and emergent relief. Establish the correct factual and legal
basis for each request. Put new affirmative relief in the notice and
proposed order; do not introduce it for the first time in reply or
argument without appropriate permission and an opportunity to respond.

Use the Family Part schedule and the full guide's mailing, receipt,
holiday, and specialized-procedure qualifications. Calendar filing and
service together. Reserve certification pages for reply before drafting;
initial and reply certifications share the movant's aggregate limit.
Documentary exhibits and newly submitted certified statements receive
different treatment under the guide.

### C. Present and implement

Request oral argument expressly where appropriate. If a tentative
decision issues, renew the request if argument remains desired. Identify
genuine material factual disputes and the evidence requiring a plenary
hearing. For emergent relief, explain the imminent harm and narrow
interim remedy with supporting proof.

Obtain the signed order and reasons. Review each requested item against
the disposition, identify reserved issues, explain compliance to the
client, and calendar performance and any reconsideration, appeal, or
stay issue from the correct trigger.

## Contested Custody and Parenting Time

### A. Screen and build the record

1\. Address child safety, domestic violence, child abuse, restraining
orders, and needed interim protections first. Screen mediation
eligibility before referral.

2\. Organize supporting and contrary evidence under the relevant
best-interests factors. Document actual caregiving, decision-making,
school continuity, needs, employment schedules, travel, and the proposed
arrangement.

3\. For modification, identify the existing arrangement and evidence of
a change affecting the child's welfare. For relocation or third-party
claims, address the applicable threshold before the merits.

4\. Identify each professional's role: treating clinician, custody
evaluator, guardian ad litem, or child's attorney. Define appointment
scope, information access, deadlines, reporting, and payment.

### B. Review therapy requests carefully

Use the updated custody guide's scientific-validity and good-cause
analysis. Address the applicable consent and age determinations for
covered reunification programs, professional qualifications,
abuse-related experience, reporting, and safeguards. Do not infer
parental fault merely from a child's resistance to contact.

The updated guide identifies unresolved implementation questions under
the 2026 amendments. Obtain case-specific directions for the statutory
private child audience and Rule 5:8-6 recording and access procedures.
Resolve the applicable procedure before presenting the child's views.

### C. Protect treatment information

Identify the clinician, patient, privilege, waiver authority, and
proposed use. A custody dispute does not automatically open all
treatment records. Assess less intrusive sources and obtain focused
rulings on any compelled review or disclosure. Keep authorization,
privilege, confidentiality, and admissibility as separate questions.

### D. Prepare findings and an enforceable order

Specify legal and physical custody, the residential schedule, holidays,
major decisions, transportation, exchanges, and safety protections. Tie
requested findings to evidence and the relevant factors. Address the
child's expressed preference and required reasons for any departure.
Identify disputed material facts requiring testimony and preserve
hearing requests and objections.

Consult the updated guide's final sections for unresolved citation,
privilege, and statutory implementation questions before relying on an
authority in a filing.

## Trial Preparation and Courtroom Practice

### A. Define the hearing and complete preparation

1\. Confirm the hearing type, issues, burdens, prior rulings, and
requested relief. Distinguish a matrimonial trial, limited plenary
hearing, domestic violence trial, and default proceeding.

2\. Reconcile the CIS, discovery, valuations, support calculations,
claimed credits, and expert reports. Follow the trial order for
exchanges and submissions; confirm any required CIS amendment or default
notice separately.

3\. Prepare witnesses and examinations by disputed fact and statutory
factor. Arrange subpoenas, custodians, experts, interpreters, and
authorized remote appearances.

4\. Prepare the exhibit index, admission foundations, complete digital
files, transcripts or translations, and playback. Identify the
underlying sources and assumptions for every financial summary.

5\. Resolve authenticity and admissibility stipulations precisely.
Obtain necessary privilege, reliability, and preliminary evidence
rulings. Do not assume a report is evidence because an expert was
court-appointed.

### B. Maintain the record during trial

Confirm issues, stipulations, sequence, time allocation, and exhibit
procedure at the outset. Distinguish testimony from counsel's argument.
Obtain an express ruling on admission and maintain a log of admitted,
limited, reserved, and excluded exhibits.

State objections and grounds when the issue arises. If evidence is
excluded, make an appropriate offer of proof and preserve the document
or testimony. If examination is limited, identify the remaining proof,
why it matters, and the additional time or remedy needed.

### C. Settlement and closing

Record resolved and reserved issues and all essential settlement terms.
Obtain knowing and voluntary assent through the appropriate procedure.
For a mediated settlement, follow the guide's signed-writing requirement
and preserve mediation privilege.

Before resting, reconcile the exhibit log, complete necessary
examinations, and obtain reserved rulings. Organize summation and
proposed findings by issue, burden, factor, evidence, and requested
relief. Use accurate transcript and admitted-exhibit references; do not
introduce new evidence in summation.

Prepare implementation terms for the judgment, including transfers,
sales, support, insurance, retirement orders, credits, and fees. Obtain
the signed judgment and reasons, explain obligations, and calendar
compliance and review deadlines.

## Advertising Review and Reference Documents

### A. Review before publication

1\. Classify the communication as general advertising, a response to an
inquiry, or unsolicited targeted outreach. Review the actual audience,
delivery method, and complete consumer-facing version.

2\. Verify firm identity, locations, attorney status, credentials, fees,
images, results, and every comparative claim. Attribute awards to the
actual recipient and review methodology, dates, and required nearby
disclosures.

3\. Check conditional requirements separately for awards, judicial
quotations, competitive-keyword landing pages, and targeted letters.
Review the advertisement and destination page, including mobile
readability.

4\. Review vendor compensation, lead sourcing, referral arrangements,
scripts, automated messages, and chatbot conduct. Attorney supervision
remains necessary when work is delegated.

5\. Obtain appropriate permission for client material and protect
confidential and prospective-client information. Review testimonials and
responses to reviews for misleading claims and disclosure risks.

### B. Approve preserve and monitor

The advertising memorandum recommends designated attorney approval of
final content and substantive vendor changes. Preserve the approved
version, substantiation, permissions, instructions, and dissemination
information. Follow its three-year retention and at least monthly
website-capture requirements, subject to the stated exceptions. Review
live content and correct problems while preserving prior versions.
