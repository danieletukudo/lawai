# Matrimonial drafting knowledge bundle

Reference examples and work product for reviewing drafting quality and designing an AI drafting workflow. Every concept is unverified until attorney review.

## Guides

- [Reading guide for the drafting examples](/guides/reading-guide.md) - Explains the two before-and-after drafting examples, how they were anonymized, and how to use them to evaluate an AI drafting workflow.

## Pension example (client is Plaintiff)

- [Pension-credit certification (earlier draft)](/examples/pension/before.md) - Plaintiff's 30-paragraph certification asking the jointly retained accountant to credit pension recoupment deductions so the same June and July obligation is not paid twice.
- [Client comments on the pension-credit certification](/examples/pension/client-comments.md) - Plaintiff's feedback on the earlier draft: the September 19, 2052 delivery date, the accountant's receipt date, an updated calculation, pending court issues, and collectible personal property.
- [Pension-credit certification (revised)](/examples/pension/after.md) - Plaintiff's 33-paragraph revision that separates the 2052 delivery to counsel from counsel's 2054 transmission to the accountant, expands the requested accounting, and seeks schedules for pending issues and property distribution.

## Support example (client is Defendant)

- [Pendente lite support certification (client's initial draft)](/examples/support/before.md) - Defendant's initial 25-paragraph account of the marital lifestyle, Plaintiff's departure, redirected payroll, and transfers from joint accounts, in support of pendente lite support.
- [Pendente lite support certification (developed)](/examples/support/after.md) - Defendant's 45-paragraph certification seeking direct payment of Schedule A and B expenses, monthly cash support, a litigation fund, exclusive occupancy, preservation orders, and financial disclosure.

## Work product

### Reconsideration opposition (August 4, 2026)

- [Plaintiff's certification opposing reconsideration](/work-product/plaintiff-certification-reconsideration-2026-08-04.md) - Plaintiff mother's certification opposing Defendant's motion to reconsider the June 11, 2026 custody and support order, with a reconciliation table showing each request was already decided.
- [Letter brief opposing reconsideration](/work-product/letter-brief-reconsideration-2026-08-04.md) - Argues under R. 4:49-2, Cummings and D'Atria that Defendant's motion identifies no palpably incorrect ruling or overlooked evidence and should be denied with fees.
