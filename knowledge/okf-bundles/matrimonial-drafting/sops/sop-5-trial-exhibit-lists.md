# SOP 5: Trial exhibit lists

## Purpose and required result

Prepare an accurate, usable list of the exhibits supporting the trial issues and a separate internal plan explaining their purpose and evidentiary needs. Every exhibit must connect to an argument, disputed fact, foundation, rebuttal, or authorized impeachment purpose. The list should not become a general demand for every document that might exist.

## Inputs and readiness

Obtain the current trial brief and issue inventory, court’s pretrial instructions, exchanged exhibit lists, existing marks, actual source files, witness list, disclosure history, and any admission stipulations or evidentiary rulings. Confirm the required court-facing columns, numbering convention, deadlines, and treatment of impeachment, rebuttal, confidential, or late-produced material.

If asked for an exhibit collection list before numbers are assigned, use stable internal identifiers and state “number to be assigned.” Do not label every missing document with an apparently final P- or D-number. A document used only for legal research does not automatically become a fact exhibit.

## Preparation procedure

1\. Extract every express exhibit reference and every material evidentiary proposition from the current brief. Map each to the issue or point, fact to prove, actual source, and proposed witness. Include adverse or impeachment material where counsel identifies a proper trial use.

2\. Inspect the actual files. Record exact date, author or custodian, relevant account or period, completeness, page count, legibility, signature status, and duplicate versions. Check attachments and conversation context.

3\. Distinguish documents received from documents requested, documents to be created, and records reserved for update. Missing records remain on an internal collection list; their absence must not be concealed by a completed-looking trial list.

4\. Decide whether records belong together. Keep coherent documents such as an agreement or report intact unless directed otherwise. Avoid one catch-all exhibit for unrelated records; do not fragment a document in a way that removes material context.

5\. Map to existing assigned numbers and court conventions. Preserve marks already exchanged or used. After numbering is settled, update every brief reference, exhibit label, index, witness outline, and electronic bookmark affected by the change.

6\. Prepare a neutral court-facing description. Include enough detail to distinguish the document from similar records without disclosing privileged analysis or asserting contested conclusions in its title.

7\. Prepare the internal evidentiary plan. Identify relevance, authentication, hearsay or other objections, necessary witness or custodian, disclosure status, and possible stipulation. A listing or exhibit sticker does not make a document admissible.

8\. Track status as the trial progresses: proposed, exchanged, marked for identification, offered, admitted, excluded, or withdrawn. Maintain a separate “received in collection” status so acquisition is not confused with admission.

9\. If assembling files is within scope, follow SOP 7 for Bates stamping trial evidence pages and preparing the final packet. Label consistently, verify complete page order, apply authorized redactions, and inspect the packet. Check current filing-system size limits rather than assuming a permanent limit. Preserve coherent documents when splitting a packet where feasible; if a split is unavoidable, label continuation and page ranges clearly.

10\. Reconcile in both directions. Every exhibit citation in the brief must resolve to a listed source or disclosed gap. Every listed exhibit must have an identified trial purpose, including a reason for any additional contextual or impeachment material.

## Separate court and internal formats

| **Court-facing list field**  | **Purpose**                                                                      |
|------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Exhibit mark                 | The actual approved or assigned identifier; provisional status must be explicit. |
| Neutral document description | Identify the record, date, author or source, and period when needed.             |
| Page or Bates range          | Locate the exact material when required or useful.                               |
| Other required fields        | Use the court’s format, including status or objections only where requested.     |

The internal working matrix should include: internal identifier; proposed or assigned trial mark; description; received or missing status; brief point or trial issue; fact and purpose; exact source and page range; foundation witness; potential objection and response; disclosure status; and admission status. Keep litigation strategy and candid assessments out of the court-facing list.

## Drafting example

Weak: “All bank statements; all tax returns; all emails.”

Acceptable approach: Identify the specific statement, account identifier as appropriately redacted, period, and issue it proves. If tracing an asset is in dispute, explain which opening balance, transfer, and later balance records are needed and why. If the brief relies on an expense, locate the invoice, proof of payment, and applicable allocation rather than gathering unrelated financial records.

For a requested exhibit list limited to the brief, do not add a broad discovery wish list. Flag a gap if the brief makes an assertion that currently has no supporting record. Counsel can then supply evidence, revise the assertion, or make an appropriate evidentiary plan.

## Acceptance checklist and handoff

- Every entry identifies a real source or clearly stated missing item and has a defined trial purpose.

- Marks, dates, descriptions, page ranges, and document versions match the actual files and brief references.

- The list distinguishes collection, exchange, identification, offering, and admission; no status is inferred.

- Duplicates, incomplete chains, missing pages, redactions, and foundation questions have been reviewed.

- The court-facing list contains no internal legal strategy or misleading argumentative titles.

- Deliver the Word exhibit list, separate internal proof and collection matrix, and assembled exhibit files only if requested. Identify all missing records and deadlines for completion.

Reject the draft if the listed exhibits cannot be located, marks conflict with existing ones, the list implies admission without a ruling or stipulation, or unrelated documents have been added without a trial purpose.
