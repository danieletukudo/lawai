# SOP 7: Trial and motion exhibit assembly

## Purpose and required result

Prepare complete, legible exhibit packets using the firm’s separate trial and motion workflows. Trial exhibit pages receive Bates identifiers, while motion exhibits are organized with numbered exhibit tabs and an index. The trial exhibit list in SOP 5 determines what belongs in the trial record and why; this SOP governs preparing, labeling, assembling, and checking the actual exhibit files.

A Bates identifier locates a specific page. An exhibit number identifies a document or approved group of documents. A tab separates one motion exhibit from the next. These identifiers perform different functions and must not be treated as interchangeable. Existing source Bates identifiers remain intact even when the same document is later used as a motion exhibit.

## Inputs and readiness

Obtain the attorney-approved exhibit list or motion exhibit ledger, actual source files and attachments, current certification or brief, prior exchanged or filed packets, existing Bates ranges and trial marks, approved redactions, and applicable court or submission instructions. Confirm whether the assignment includes electronic files, printed binders, or both.

For trial, establish the approved Bates prefix, number format, last identifier used, scope of the sequence, and any existing identifiers to preserve. For motions, establish the exhibit numbers, index format, tab labels, and whether a combined packet or separate exhibit files are required. Check current submission specifications, file-size limits, confidentiality treatment, and intended recipients; do not assume a prior matter’s requirements apply.

## Choose the correct workflow

| **Item**             | **Trial exhibits**                                                                                                      | **Motion exhibits**                                                                                                                               |
|----------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

## Common preparation steps

1\. Freeze a working exhibit ledger based on the latest approved draft. Assign a stable internal identifier to each item and record the source filename, version, date, description, page count, intended use, and trial mark or motion exhibit number. Identify missing records before assembly.

2\. Preserve evidentiary originals unchanged. Assemble from working copies. Keep native files and original metadata where relevant. Do not overwrite original production files, remove existing Bates identifiers, or imply that a converted copy is the native original.

3\. Inspect every page and attachment for completeness, legibility, orientation, and correct order. Verify signature and continuation pages. Include the necessary context for email or message chains. Use excerpts only when authorized and describe them accurately; do not present an excerpt as the complete record.

4\. Remove accidental duplicate working copies, drafting comments, and internal strategy materials from the external packet. Retain an intentional duplicate where needed for a distinct approved purpose and cross-reference it in the ledger. Do not silently omit an exhibit the attorney directed be included.

5\. Apply only authorized redactions to the release copy. Use an actual redaction process, not a visible black shape that leaves recoverable text underneath. Check searchable text, annotations, attachments, and metadata as appropriate. Preserve the unredacted original separately and identify which version is being used.

6\. Convert documentary copies into a consistent, readable PDF format where appropriate. Use optical character recognition (OCR) for scans where feasible and verify important text against the image. Preserve color when it conveys evidence. Do not crop, resize, flatten, or compress in a way that removes content or materially reduces readability.

7\. Treat audio, video, spreadsheets with material formulas, and other native evidence separately where needed. Preserve the native item, identify its filename and intended delivery method in the ledger, and cross-reference any transcript, still image, or PDF representation. Do not imply that a placeholder sheet replaces the actual evidence.

## Trial workflow for Bates stamping

1\. Confirm the scope of the numbering sequence and reserve the next unused number in the Bates control register. Use the approved prefix and fixed-width sequential digits. The prefix must distinguish the intended matter or production set; a new file should not restart a sequence already in use.

2\. Inventory any pages already bearing Bates identifiers. Preserve those identifiers. Use the existing range and a cross-reference where sufficient. Add another identifier only under the approved convention and without obscuring the original. Do not silently give an exchanged page a new identity.

3\. Put new evidence pages in the approved document order before assigning their Bates numbers. Stamp each new documentary evidence page once within the approved sequence. Index sheets, tab sheets, and binder covers are administrative pages and do not consume evidence-page Bates numbers under the default firm workflow.

4\. Position the stamp consistently in an unobstructed margin and inspect every page. Use a legible size and contrast. Do not cover dates, signatures, account details, existing page numbers, prior Bates identifiers, or other evidence. If needed, add margin to the working copy rather than shrinking the source until it is difficult to read.

5\. Record the first and last Bates identifier for each document, page count, source, assigned trial exhibit mark, and any exceptions. Verify that the numeric range matches the actual number of newly stamped pages. Account for intentionally retained blank pages; do not insert unexplained blanks or conceal missing pages by renumbering.

6\. Generate or update the trial exhibit list and Bates cross-reference. Keep the exhibit mark separate from the Bates range. Update the brief, witness materials, and other references only after the range has been checked. An exhibit can span many Bates pages.

7\. Preserve identifiers through later revisions. Add new pages using the approved next available sequence and record the supplement. Do not insert them into an existing range by shifting numbers on pages already exchanged or cited. Use an attorney-approved insertion convention or separate supplemental range if required, with a clear cross-reference. Never reuse a retired identifier for a different page.

8\. If a complete renumbering is specifically approved, retain the prior version and an old-to-new cross-reference, then correct all affected references and identify the superseded set for the appropriate recipients. Do not mix old and new sets in one trial binder.

Illustrative sequence only: a three-page document receives SAMPLE000001 through SAMPLE000003; the next two-page document receives SAMPLE000004 through SAMPLE000005. A preceding index and separator sheets do not change those evidence-page identifiers. The trial exhibit marks remain whatever the attorney or court assigns.

## Motion workflow for numbered tabs and an index

1\. Build the motion exhibit ledger from the actual certification, brief, and attorney directions. Match each reference, such as **Exhibit 1**, to one defined document or approved group. Resolve duplicate numbers, missing exhibits, and inconsistent descriptions before building the packet.

2\. Order exhibits in the approved sequence. For a new packet, use the intended citation sequence unless directed otherwise. Preserve numbers already filed or served when preparing a supplement or reply; identify whether the supplement continues an existing sequence or uses another approved designation rather than guessing.

3\. Prepare an index at the front of the packet or in the designated separate file. For each entry, include the exhibit number, neutral document description, date or covered period, and final packet location when a combined packet is used. State whether the location refers to the tab sheet or the first evidence page and apply that convention consistently. The preferred working index identifies the first evidence page, with bookmarks opening the corresponding tab.

4\. Insert a clearly labeled separator sheet immediately before each exhibit, using bold labels such as **Exhibit 1** and **Exhibit 2**. For physical binders, use matching numbered divider tabs. For electronic packets, divider pages and bookmarks provide navigation; a decorative tab graphic alone does not locate the exhibit.

5\. Keep each exhibit’s evidence pages together and in their original order. Include approved attachments and any needed context. An MSA or other coherent agreement should remain together where feasible; do not split its pages between unrelated exhibit groups for convenience.

6\. Add consistent packet pagination if the combined format calls for it. Count the index and separator pages according to the chosen packet convention, but do not confuse that count with the source document’s printed page numbers or any existing Bates identifiers. Generate the final index locations after the index and tabs have been inserted.

7\. Add electronic bookmarks using the exhibit number and a concise description where supported. Verify that every bookmark reaches the correct exhibit tab and that every index entry and filing reference resolves to the intended document. Recalculate packet locations whenever an exhibit, index page, or separator changes.

8\. Check the certification and brief against the completed packet in both directions. Every visible exhibit reference must have the matching exhibit, and every packet exhibit must have an approved purpose. Confirm bold numbered references and replace provisional placeholders before the release stage requiring completion.

If the motion uses documents already Bates-stamped in discovery or for trial, retain those identifiers for cross-reference while still organizing the motion packet with its numbered tabs and index. Do not remove or replace source stamps merely to make the packet appear uniform.

## Suggested control records

| **Record**                    | **Minimum fields**                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
|-------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Bates control register        | Matter or set; prefix and digit format; next unused number; assigned ranges; source documents and versions; date assigned; supplements, retired ranges, and exceptions.                                                  |
| Trial exhibit cross-reference | Trial mark or provisional identifier; neutral description; date or period; page count; first and last Bates identifiers or identified existing ranges; source file; preparation and admission status in separate fields. |
| Motion exhibit index          | Exhibit number; neutral description; date or period; final packet location under the stated convention; continuation volume or file where applicable.                                                                    |
| Assembly ledger               | Internal identifier; source and version; original and final page count; redaction or excerpt status; output filename; missing items; reviewer and completion date.                                                       |

The external index must not contain privileged analysis, candid objection assessments, or internal collection notes. Keep those in the working ledger and proof map.

## Packet assembly and final review

1\. Build the combined packet or separate files in the required submission format. Name volumes and exhibits consistently so the recipient can identify their sequence. If size requires multiple volumes, split at exhibit boundaries where feasible, provide a volume map, and preserve existing Bates identifiers and exhibit numbers. If a coherent document must be split, clearly label its continuation and ranges.

2\. Reconcile evidence-page counts with source files, and separately account for added index and tab pages. Review blank pages and duplicates deliberately. A matching total alone does not prove that the correct pages were included.

3\. For trial copies, check every Bates identifier for uniqueness, sequence, completeness, visibility, and correct document mapping. Review first and last pages of every range, plus every intervening page for misplaced stamps or covered content. Identify all gaps rather than silently closing them.

4\. For motion copies, verify every tab, index entry, exhibit reference, packet location, bookmark, and file boundary. Confirm that divider pages do not substitute for missing evidence and that page locations reflect the final assembled packet.

5\. Open the actual final files after any compression, redaction, splitting, or export. Inspect every page for orientation, legibility, missing content, overlays, and correct order. Test navigation and search on important records. Check that external copies contain the intended redacted versions and no internal working material.

6\. Record the prepared version and any unresolved item. Keep filing and service copies consistent except for specifically authorized confidential or sealed variations, which must be identified and controlled. Obtain the required attorney release approval; packet preparation alone does not authorize filing, service, or a claim that trial evidence has been admitted.

## Acceptance checklist and handoff

- Trial evidence pages bear the approved Bates identifiers, with no unexplained duplicates, gaps, or overwritten source identifiers; the range register and trial exhibit cross-reference agree.

- Motion exhibits have correctly numbered tabs and a complete index that matches the actual certification or brief and final packet locations.

- Originals are preserved, approved redactions are effective, and all evidence pages and attachments are complete, legible, and correctly ordered.

- Exhibit marks, Bates identifiers, source-page numbers, and packet pagination remain distinct and accurately cross-referenced.

- The final files and any physical binders use the same approved exhibit structure. Supplements and replacement sets are clearly identified.

- Deliver the prepared exhibit files or binders, the applicable external index, and the internal control ledger. List missing items and exceptions separately. Retain the source originals and prior exchanged versions within the matter record.

Reject the packet if Bates identifiers collide, stamps obscure evidence, a motion tab or index points to the wrong document, an exhibit referenced in the filing is absent, page ranges are inaccurate, redactions are ineffective, or a working copy is mistaken for the approved release copy.
