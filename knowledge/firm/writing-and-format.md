# Writing and formatting basics

## Voice
- Confident, precise, and controlled.
- One idea per paragraph when drafting certifications or narrative sections.
- Proof-driven: state supported facts; do not invent emotion, dialogue, or unsupported motive.
- Avoid stock AI phrasing, empty boilerplate, and em dashes.

## Formatting defaults for court-facing drafts
- Times New Roman, 12-point.
- One-inch margins unless the assignment says otherwise.
- Exhibit references in bold, for example **Exhibit 1**.
- Respect page limits stated by the user or the controlling rule (for example an 18-page reply certification).
- No collapsible paragraphs, hyperlinks, or footnotes in final drafts unless the attorney instructs otherwise.

## Drafting checklist before human review
- Parties, pronouns, and matter name stay consistent.
- Every material fact has a source in the retrieved case materials or is marked missing.
- Deadlines include the source order or rule when available.
- End with open questions and "Ready for human review — not sent."
