# Cross-matter controls

Use these controls on every assignment:

1. Identify the docket, procedural posture, disputed issues, existing orders, and specific relief before choosing a form or procedure.
2. Calendar the applicable rule deadlines and case dates. Confirm any extension, adjournment, or schedule change with the court or attorney.
3. Connect each requested result to the required legal showing, competent evidence, and a workable proposed order.
4. Review confidentiality, privilege, redactions, signatures, service, and filing requirements before release. Preserve the final submission and proof of service.
5. After a ruling, obtain the signed order and reasons. Explain obligations to the client. Calendar implementation and review deadlines.

Never mix facts, exhibits, or deadlines from another matter into the current assignment.
